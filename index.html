Our Story

A passion for origin, small-batch roasting, and warm prose.

Founded in 2018, **Bean & Prose** was born out of a desire to create a sanctuary for coffee lovers—a place where quality ingredients meet mindful conversation.

We source micro-lots directly from small farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Brazil, honoring the farmers' craft and delivering distinct flavor profiles with every pour.

The Craft of Extraction

Signature Brewing Methods

Select your brewing preference to highlight different subtle flavor notes in your cup.

Hario V60

Conical pour-over with spiral ribs. Enhances bright acidity, delicate floral aromas, and a light body.

Profile: Light & Crisp

Aeropress

Smooth immersion with gentle air pressure. Yields rich sweetness, fuller body, and low acidity.

Profile: Rich & Sweet

French Press

Full immersion extraction without paper filters. Retains natural oils for a velvety, robust texture.

Profile: Bold & Velvety

Espresso Bar

High-pressure extraction producing concentrated flavor topped with a dense golden crema layer.

Profile: Intense & Complex
